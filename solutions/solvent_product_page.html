Our approach

GST reconciliation is where we start. Not where we stop.

Every workflow we add — TDS, accounts payable, month-end close — is judged the same way: does it hold up against what a careful accountant would actually do, not just what looks automated. We'd rather be slow and trusted than fast and wrong.

Underneath, GST and TDS are a compliance plugin on a core engine that doesn't change per country — the same reconciliation, exception-routing, and approval logic. India is where we're proving it first, not where the platform ends.

Security & audit

Built to be audited, not just trusted

This is compliance work — the standard is what a CA firm's own auditor, or a GST officer, would expect to see, not just what a typical SaaS product ships with.

Encryption

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Every document, ledger entry, and reconciliation record encrypted end to end.

Identity & access

Role-based access per firm and per client, 2FA on every account — a firm's junior staff sees only what their role permits.

Audit logging

Every read, edit, approval, and rejection immutably logged with actor, timestamp, and before/after state — not just a summary report.

Data residency & isolation

Hosted in India, per-client data isolation by design — Client A's books are never reachable from Client B's workspace.

Maker-checker, enforced

The approval step is a system-level gate, not a policy suggestion — the AI cannot mark its own work as filed or approved.

Certification roadmap

SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 targeted post-seed, as the customer base that requires them is onboarded — not claimed before they're earned.

This is the standard we're building to before asking a firm to trust us with a live filing — not a claim that it's all live today.

Questions

Answers to what you'd actually ask

Will Solvent ever file anything without a CA's sign-off?

No. Every GST return, TDS filing, and vendor payment is prepared as a draft and stays a draft until a human — your CA or an independent CA partner — explicitly approves it. Solvent has no path to file or pay on its own.

Does Solvent train on my data?

No. Each client's data is isolated by design and is never used to train a shared model. What Solvent learns from your corrections stays specific to your books.

Do I still need a CA once I'm using Solvent?

For filing sign-off, yes — Solvent doesn't replace that professional relationship. What it removes is the manual data-entry and reconciliation work underneath it, so your CA's time (and your bill) shifts from typing to reviewing.

What happens when GST or TDS rules change?

Compliance logic is maintained centrally and updated as rules change, so you're not relying on a static tool that goes stale after the next notification.

Is my data safe, and does it stay in India?

Yes — data residency in India, aligned with the DPDP Act 2023, with per-client isolation and a full audit log of every action taken on your books.

Can my existing CA use this alongside me?

Yes — Solvent is built to make an existing CA relationship faster and cheaper to maintain, not to route around it.
