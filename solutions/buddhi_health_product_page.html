THE VISIT YOU ALREADY KNOW

Two minutes, a fever, three possible causes — and a note that gets shorter every time volume goes up.

The patient describes their symptoms in Telugu, switches to English mid-sentence, and the doctor is already thinking about the next twenty people in the waiting room. Whatever gets typed in that window has to carry the differential, the plan, and the record — in a system built for single-language, unhurried input. Tools built somewhere else don't fix this: they carry someone else's guidelines, someone else's tests, and English-only listening into a visit that runs on none of those.

THE SHIFT

Describe the visit. Get the draft — never the decision.

01

Listens in the language actually spoken

Code-switched, multi-language, real-time — built around how Indian consultations actually sound, not accented English.

02

Reasons like it trained here

Grounded in India-first guidelines, tests, and formulary data — never a foreign default quietly standing in for local practice.

03

Sees more than the conversation

Labs, imaging, vitals, medication and visit history — where available — inform the same picture as the transcript, not a separate silo.

04

Never finalizes without the doctor

Every item is a draft. Nothing reaches the record, and nothing is prescribed, until a registered practitioner says so.

05

Follows the patient, with their consent

A history from any ABDM-linked facility — not just ones running Buddhi — joins the same picture, so a first visit doesn't start from a blank page.

06

Add to your own record

A prescription or report from a visit we couldn't reach any other way — photographed and added by the patient, mapped straight into the same history.
