Our approach

Chat is where we start. Not where we stop.

Every feature we add — cart recovery, guided-discovery quizzes, replenishment reminders — is judged the same way: does it hold up as an honest answer to a real shopper, not just something that looks automated. We'd rather under-promise on what the bot can do than have it improvise an answer that costs you a return.

The "flat" in FlatChat is a promise about honesty, not an unlimited claim. Pricing has two honest parts: a fixed fee for the platform, and a component that only grows when it's tied to something you actually got — a resolved question, a recovered cart — never a meter running quietly on raw message volume.

Underneath, the Shopify-specific pieces — catalog sync, order lookups, checkout events — sit on a support engine that isn't tied to one platform. Shopify is where we're proving it first, not where the product ends.

Questions

Answers to what you'd actually ask

Will FlatChat ever cancel an order or issue a refund without my approval?

No. FlatChat can look up and explain — order status, shipping, stock — but any action with money or inventory attached, like refunds, cancellations, or address changes, is drafted for your team to approve, never executed on its own.

Where does payment go when someone buys inside the chat?

Straight to Shopify's own checkout. FlatChat builds the product card and cart, then hands off to Shopify's PCI-compliant checkout for payment — card numbers never pass through FlatChat's systems at any point.

Does FlatChat train on my store's data?

No. Each store's catalog, conversation history, and quiz responses are isolated and never used to train a shared model.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

It says so, and hands the conversation to your inbox or Slack with full context — it doesn't guess at a spec or a policy it isn't sure about.

How does "fixed plus outcome-based" pricing actually work?

You pay a small fixed fee for the platform itself — catalog sync, the widget, order lookups, objection handling — plus a component that scales with outcomes actually achieved, like a resolved question, a recovered cart, or a purchase made inside the chat. A reply that didn't do anything doesn't add to the bill. We'll walk through the exact structure and a number for your store when you contact us.

What counts as an "outcome"?

Things you'd count as a win yourself — a support question resolved without a human, a cart recovered after a nudge, an order placed through a chat-based product card, a guided-discovery quiz that ends in a purchase. Not raw message count, and not a reply that didn't lead anywhere. We agree on the specific definitions with you before you're billed on them.

Why is this better for me than a flat per-seat or per-resolution plan?

A flat per-resolution plan bills you the same whether the bot helped or not — you're carrying all the risk if it underperforms. Splitting the price into a fixed base and an outcome component moves some of that risk onto us: our upside only grows when yours does, so a slow month costs you less automatically, and there's no incentive on our side to inflate reply counts just to run up your bill.

What do I need to install it?

Just a Shopify store and a few minutes — FlatChat reads your product catalog and order data through the Shopify Admin API, no separate account or migration.

Can I customize how it sounds?

Yes — tone, greeting, and escalation rules are all configurable, so it sounds like your store, not a generic bot.
