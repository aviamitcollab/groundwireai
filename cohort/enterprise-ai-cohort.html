03Fit Check

Who this program is for.

We'd rather tell you now than have you find out in Week 3.

A strong fit if you're —
  • A backend, platform, or ML engineer moving into GenAI work inside your own company
  • A data scientist ready to go from notebooks to a real, deployed, monitored system
  • Part of an internal AI/platform team being upskilled together
  • 3–4+ years of general software engineering experience, no customer-facing background needed
Not the right fit if you —
  • Need discovery-call, proposal, or customer-facing skills — see our Forward Deployed Engineer track instead
  • Are looking for a theory-only or prompting-only course
  • Want a certificate without shipping a real, working system
